# Common account suggestions

Approved 2026-09-21: one editable Japanese account catalog for companies and sole proprietors. These are common bookkeeping labels, not a universal tax-law-mandated chart or a determination of deductibility. No entity selector or new paid provider.

Sources checked 2026-09-21: [NTA general expense examples](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/kojin_jigyo/kichou03.pdf), [NTA business expenses](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2210.htm), [NTA purpose-dependent entertainment rules](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/hojin/5265.htm). The individual-business guide supplies examples, not corporate-specific tax conclusions.

- Explicit purchase/service words support candidates. A convenience-store/pharmacy name alone is insufficient; ignore promotional/footer text. One clear match is provisional; multiple matches offer alternatives. Never use miscellaneous expense just to fill a blank.
- Food/drink requires purpose review between meeting, entertainment and welfare. Fuel is provisionally vehicle expense in this app's common chart, and remains editable. Parcels are shipping; stamps/postcards are communications.
- Potential equipment requires expense/asset review without a receipt-total threshold. Tax rates, deductibility and business/private allocation are separate decisions.
- Store canonical Japanese names, show translated labels, allow custom names, preserve revision/version checks.
- Cached OCR may be reclassified without another paid call. Preserve dates, amounts, originals/files and user-edited values; skip trash and check versions.
